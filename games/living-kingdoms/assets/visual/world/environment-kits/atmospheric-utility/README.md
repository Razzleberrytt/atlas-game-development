# Atmospheric Utility Props

Held, presentation-only lighting and utility dressing for frontier, mine, industrial, and corrupted-wilderness spaces.

The kit provides a hanging work lamp, broken floodlight, generator shell, and lantern post. Their sockets are presentation hooks only: they do not create power, interaction, fuel, hazards, or other gameplay authority.

All models remain runtime-unmapped and Studio-unapproved until a later placement/integration pass.
