# Corruption Growth Kit

Held, presentation-only source models for corrupted wilderness, mine, and abandoned-industry dressing.

The kit provides four complementary silhouette/readability pieces: `ResinGrowthClusterA` for low hostile ground spread, `CorruptedRootArchA` for traversal-scale framing, `CorruptionGroundSpireA` for vertical corruption landmarks, and `SporeLanternGrowthA` for emissive fungal-style accents. Final meshes may replace the primitive geometry without changing semantic IDs or registered source-model paths.

These assets own no corruption-state truth, hazards, damage, gathering, loot, interaction, AI, quests, progression, or spawn authority. Their VFX/audio attachments are presentation sockets only. They remain runtime-unmapped and Studio-unapproved until a later integration pass.
