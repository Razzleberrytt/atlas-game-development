# Cave + Ritual Environment Kit

Held, presentation-only source models for subterranean ritual dressing across mine and corrupted-wilderness spaces.

The kit is modular: `CaveRibArchA` frames cave thresholds and tunnel transitions, `RitualStoneAltarA` provides a readable focal landmark, `RunicStonePillarA` builds repeated ritual silhouettes, and `OfferingBrazierA` supplies ambient foreground dressing. Final meshes may replace the primitive geometry without changing semantic IDs.

These assets own no ritual mechanics, encounter state, hazards, traversal truth, loot, interaction, AI, progression, gathering, or rewards. Their attachments are presentation/assembly sockets only. They remain runtime-unmapped and Studio-unapproved until a later integration pass.
