# Industrial Rail + Debris Kit

Held, presentation-only source models for abandoned industrial and mine dressing.

The kit is intentionally modular: repeat `RailTrackSectionA` for intact stretches, splice in `BrokenRailBendA` where decay should interrupt the line, use `SignalPostA` as a readable landmark, and use `CollapsedPipeStackA` to break silhouettes and create industrial clutter. Final meshes may replace the primitive geometry without changing semantic IDs.

These assets own no route truth, collision policy decisions beyond their registered presentation contract, gathering, hazards, loot, interaction, AI, or progression. They remain runtime-unmapped and Studio-unapproved until a later integration pass.
