# Ruined Frontier Structure Kit

Held, project-original modular source geometry for composing abandoned frontier buildings.

The kit intentionally favors **fragments over whole prefab houses**. `CollapsedShackCornerA`, `BoardedDoorwayA`, `BrokenRoofFrameA`, and `RuinedWindowWallA` can be rotated, mirrored in authored placement, combined with camp/industrial props, and spaced with missing sections to create many distinct silhouettes from a small source set.

Production principles:

- preserve obvious gaps, collapse, and asymmetry rather than making every ruin rectangular;
- use doorway/window pieces to create readable traversal openings, but gameplay/pathfinding authority remains elsewhere;
- roofs should be incomplete enough to admit strong sky/light silhouettes;
- mix wood with sparse rusted-metal repairs rather than clean construction;
- combine with dead roots, tarp/camp pieces, crates, and mine/industry debris to tell occupation/abandonment stories;
- keep individual modules streamable and replaceable instead of welding a settlement into one giant model.

These files are held presentation source only. They do not define collision policy beyond registry metadata, enemy cover logic, loot placement, traversal authority, quests, interactions, streaming radii, or runtime spawning. Final meshes/materials may replace primitive geometry without changing semantic IDs.
