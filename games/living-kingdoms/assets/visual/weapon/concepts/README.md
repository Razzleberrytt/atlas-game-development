# Future Weapon Visual Concepts — Wave 1

Held visual-only blockouts for future firearm art exploration. These concepts intentionally have no canonical firearm IDs, damage, cadence, ammo, recoil, rarity, unlock, economy, or crafting balance.

Wave 1 explores four deliberately different silhouettes: the Gravesend heavy revolver, Widowmaker lever rifle, Harrow industrial auto-shotgun, and Cinder scavenged carbine.

A later gameplay-authority decision may promote, rename, reject, or substantially redesign any concept. Until then they remain runtime-unmapped and Studio-unapproved source-art inventory.
