# Bloater Rot V1

Held, presentation-only source blockout for `enemy.presentation.bloater.rot_v1`.

## Visual identity

The Bloater should read as an unstable pressure hazard at a glance: oversized asymmetric translucent sacs, a dragging side-weight silhouette, a low head buried under the torso mass, and one visibly vulnerable pressure panel. The larger left sac intentionally dominates the shape so its lurching weight reads before texture detail exists.

## Iteration boundary

A final mesh/rig may replace this blockout, but it should preserve the asymmetric pressure-sac silhouette, dragging side mass, translucent swelling language, readable pressure weak point, and existing attack-tell presentation. This source owns no AI, damage, explosion logic, spawning, rewards, or status effects and remains unmapped until a later integration pass.
